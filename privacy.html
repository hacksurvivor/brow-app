Privacy Policy

Brow · effective 1 October 2026

Brow does not collect, store or share personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Everything Brow shows is read on your Mac and stays on your Mac: your Shelf items, screenshots, settings and readings.

Services Brow talks to

When you turn them on, Brow talks directly to these services on your behalf:

  • Anthropic (api.anthropic.com), to read your Claude usage limits with your Claude Code login, read-only.
  • The news outlets and quote providers you choose, to load headlines and prices.
  • YouTube, to play videos you open in the notch.

Your Claude Code login

Brow reads it from your Keychain only after macOS asks you and you allow it. It is never stored elsewhere, logged, or sent anywhere but Anthropic. You can turn this off in Settings › Agents.
